Step 2 • Audience and Context

Sharing is not simply public or private.

The safest decision depends on what the information is, who will receive it, why it is needed, and what may happen after it is shared.

Usually safer to share

General information that does not identify, locate, embarrass, or expose you or another person.

Share with caution

Photos, schedules, achievements, school details, and personal stories that may reveal more than intended.

Keep private

Passwords, codes, IDs, exact addresses, confidential documents, medical details, and information shared in confidence.

Step 3 • App Permissions

Access should match the task.

Permissions are not harmless checkboxes. They may allow an app to collect location, contacts, photos, microphone input, camera data, or files.

01

Location

Ask whether the app truly needs precise or continuous location access.

  • Prefer “while using” when appropriate.
  • Disable background access if unnecessary.
02

Contacts

Access may expose information belonging to other people, not only you.

  • Decline if contact syncing is not essential.
  • Review uploaded address books.
03

Camera and microphone

Allow access only when a feature genuinely requires recording or scanning.

  • Close access after use.
  • Watch for unexpected indicators.
04

Photos and files

Choose selected files instead of full-library access when the platform allows it.

  • Check what will be uploaded.
  • Remove hidden or identifying details.
05

Notifications

Notifications may reveal private messages on a locked screen.

  • Hide sensitive previews.
  • Disable unnecessary alerts.
06

Account connections

Signing in with another platform may connect profile data and permissions.

  • Review linked applications.
  • Remove connections you no longer use.

Step 4 • Digital Footprints and Cloud Sharing

Visibility can continue after you press share.

Posts, links, files, comments, and location details may remain searchable, downloadable, cached, forwarded, or visible to people outside the original audience.

Live location

Sharing exact real-time location can reveal routines, travel, and when a home may be unattended.

Cloud links

“Anyone with the link” may allow access beyond the intended class or group.

Photo details

Uniforms, house numbers, IDs, screens, name tags, and metadata may reveal identity or location.

Old posts

Past content may still affect reputation, safety, or future opportunities.

Tagged content

Other people may connect your name to content you did not create or approve.

Shared documents

Comments, revision history, hidden sheets, and file names may expose more than the visible page.

Step 5 • Respect and Recovery

Privacy includes other people, too.

Responsible sharing means considering consent, context, dignity, and harm before posting or forwarding someone else's information.

Ask permissionGet consent before posting identifiable images, stories, or private messages.
Limit detailsRemove names, faces, locations, or documents when they are not necessary.
Do not forward harmAvoid spreading screenshots, rumors, private chats, or embarrassing content.
Act after a mistakeDelete where possible, notify affected people, adjust settings, and report serious exposure.
