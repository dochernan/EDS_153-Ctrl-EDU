Fast Reference

Two guides worth remembering

Before sharing information

  1. Pause and identify what the content reveals.
  2. Check whether another person is included.
  3. Choose the smallest appropriate audience.
  4. Remove unnecessary personal details.
  5. Confirm that access can be changed or revoked.

After a privacy or security incident

  1. Stop the risky activity.
  2. Secure the account, file, or device.
  3. Preserve useful evidence.
  4. Report to the correct person or service.
  5. Recover, review, and improve protections.

KALASAG Quick Reference

Before Sharing

  1. Identify personal or sensitive information.
  2. Check the audience and purpose.
  3. Remove unnecessary details.
  4. Ask consent when another person is involved.
  5. Use the least access necessary.

Account Security

  • Use unique long passphrases.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Review active sessions and recovery methods.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.

Spot the Trap

  • Check the sender and destination.
  • Be cautious of urgency, secrecy, fear, or rewards.
  • Verify using an official channel.
  • Do not open unexpected files or login links.

Incident Response

  1. Pause.
  2. Protect.
  3. Preserve evidence.
  4. Report.
  5. Recover and review.
