Module 5 • Privacy in the Age of AI

Before you prompt, protect the data.

AI tools can help you learn, create, and solve problems—but every prompt, upload, and conversation may involve personal or confidential information. This module will help you use AI without exposing data that should remain protected.

Step 2 • Know the Data

AI does not need your whole story.

Only provide the minimum information needed for the task. A useful prompt can usually be written without names, account details, private conversations, health information, grades, addresses, or confidential school records.

Safer information

  • Fictional names and situations
  • General questions without identifying details
  • Public information from authorized sources
  • Short excerpts with private details removed
  • Aggregated or anonymized data

Keep protected

  • Passwords, verification codes, or recovery answers
  • Student numbers, IDs, addresses, and phone numbers
  • Private messages, counseling notes, or medical details
  • Unreleased grades, disciplinary records, or confidential files
  • Another person's image, voice, or information without permission
Quick decision

You want AI to improve a classmate's résumé. Which approach is safest?

Step 3 • Safer Prompting

Rewrite the task, not the identity.

Anonymization means removing or replacing details that can directly or indirectly identify a person.

Remove identifiersNames, usernames, email addresses, student numbers, faces, and contact details.
Generalize contextReplace exact schools, locations, dates, and positions with broader descriptions.
Reduce the contentSubmit only the paragraph, table, or section needed for the task.
Use placeholdersUse labels such as Student A, School X, or [email removed].

Step 4 • Review Every Upload

A file may reveal more than you expect.

Documents and images can contain names, comments, metadata, tracked changes, hidden sheets, faces, signatures, locations, and background details.

DOC

Documents

Check headers, comments, revision history, file names, author fields, and hidden text.

IMG

Images

Look for faces, IDs, screens, addresses, uniforms, location clues, and private surroundings.

DATA

Spreadsheets

Remove names, emails, identifiers, hidden columns, and unnecessary individual records.

Upload checklist

A spreadsheet contains survey responses from classmates. You want AI to summarize the patterns. What should you do first?

Anonymize and minimize

Step 5 • Make Informed Choices

Ask four questions before using AI.

Responsible use begins before the prompt is submitted.

What data?Does the content include personal, sensitive, confidential, or third-party information?
Why this tool?Can the task be completed without sharing the data or by using a safer alternative?
Which rules?Does the school, teacher, organization, or platform permit this use?
What if exposed?Would the person or organization be harmed if the content were seen by others?
