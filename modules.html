Module 1

Understanding AI in School and Everyday Life

Most surveyed learners already use AI regularly. This module establishes a shared understanding of what AI can do, what it cannot do, and why responsible human judgment remains important.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain artificial intelligence and generative AI using simple examples.
  • Identify common uses of AI in school, social media, communication, and future workplaces.
  • Describe the benefits and limitations of AI.
  • Explain why AI should support rather than replace human thinking.

Suggested Activities

  • “Where Do You Encounter AI?” interactive activity
  • Benefits-versus-risks classification quiz
  • Short animated introduction to generative AI
  • Reflection: “How do I currently use AI?”

Module 2

Think Before You Trust

Survey results showed that many learners use AI but have limited confidence in verifying its outputs. This module focuses on fact-checking, source evaluation, and critical thinking.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify inaccurate, unsupported, or misleading AI-generated claims.
  • Recognize fabricated references and AI hallucinations.
  • Compare AI-generated information with credible sources.
  • Apply the Stop–Check–Verify–Revise process.

Suggested Activities

  • Find the errors in an AI-generated response
  • Credible-versus-unreliable source activity
  • Fact-checking challenge
  • AI response revision exercise

Module 3

Responsible AI-Assisted Research and Writing

AI-assisted research and writing was the most requested area for improvement. This module teaches learners to use AI as a support tool while maintaining their own thinking, voice, and responsibility.

Learning Objectives

  • Use AI for brainstorming and developing research questions.
  • Evaluate AI-generated outlines and summaries.
  • Verify sources and factual claims before use.
  • Revise AI suggestions using the learner’s own language and judgment.

Suggested Activities

  • Improve a weak AI prompt
  • Revise an AI-generated paragraph
  • Identify unsupported citations
  • Compare original and AI-assisted writing

Module 4

Academic Integrity and AI Transparency

The survey revealed uncertainty about whether unacknowledged AI use is academically dishonest. This module helps learners distinguish assistance from substitution and understand responsible authorship.

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish acceptable AI assistance from plagiarism and dishonest submission.
  • Explain why learners remain responsible for their final output.
  • Identify situations that require AI acknowledgment.
  • Write a simple AI-use disclosure statement.

Suggested Activities

  • “Responsible or Irresponsible?” scenario game
  • Assistance-versus-substitution classification
  • AI acknowledgment writing activity
  • Academic integrity decision challenge

Module 5

Privacy, Safety, and Digital Responsibility

Personal data and security were the most frequently reported concerns. This module teaches learners to protect themselves and others when using AI platforms.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify personal and sensitive information.
  • Explain why private information should not be entered into public AI tools.
  • Recognize risks involving deepfakes, manipulated media, and impersonation.
  • Apply safe and respectful digital practices.

Suggested Activities

  • Safe-to-share versus unsafe-to-share activity
  • Privacy risk scenario
  • Deepfake and manipulated-content discussion
  • Personal AI safety checklist

Module 6

Responsible AI for Career Preparation

This module connects responsible AI literacy to future employment, communication, problem-solving, and career-readiness skills.

Learning Objectives

  • Use AI to explore careers and required skills.
  • Improve a résumé without inventing qualifications or experiences.
  • Prepare professional emails and interview responses.
  • Evaluate AI-generated career advice for accuracy and relevance.

Suggested Activities

  • Improve an AI-generated résumé profile
  • Rewrite a professional email
  • Practice interview questions using AI
  • Create a responsible AI-assisted career output

Final Performance Task

Apply responsible AI use to a career-related task.

Learners will use an AI tool to assist with a career-related output while documenting how they verified, revised, and took responsibility for the final work.

  1. Select a career-related task.
  2. Write and submit the original AI prompt.
  3. Save the initial AI-generated response.
  4. Verify important information using credible sources.
  5. Revise the output using personal judgment and voice.
  6. Explain how AI was used responsibly.
