Module 1

Understanding AI in School and Everyday Life

Artificial intelligence is already part of many tools you use every day. In this module, you will explore what AI can do, what its limitations are, and why human thinking remains essential.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • Explain AI and generative AI using simple examples.
  • Identify common uses of AI in school and daily life.
  • Describe the benefits and limitations of AI.
  • Explain why AI should support, not replace, human thinking.

🎮 What You Will Do

  • Identify AI tools you already encounter.
  • Sort examples into AI benefits and risks.
  • Watch a short introduction to generative AI.
  • Reflect on how you currently use AI.
Continue to Module 2

Module 2

Think Before You Trust

AI can provide answers quickly, but those answers are not always accurate. Learn how to pause, investigate, and verify information before accepting or sharing it.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • Identify inaccurate or unsupported claims.
  • Recognize AI hallucinations and fabricated references.
  • Compare AI responses with credible sources.
  • Apply the Stop–Check–Verify–Revise process.

🧩 What You Will Do

  • Find errors in an AI-generated response.
  • Compare reliable and unreliable sources.
  • Complete a fact-checking challenge.
  • Revise an inaccurate AI response.

Verification Strategy

Stop before you copy.

Use this four-step process whenever AI provides information for school or personal use.

  1. Stop and read the response carefully.
  2. Check the claims, dates, and references.
  3. Verify the information using credible sources.
  4. Revise the response using your own judgment.

Module 3

Responsible AI-Assisted Research and Writing

AI can help you brainstorm, organize ideas, and improve your writing. The final output, however, should still reflect your understanding, ideas, and personal voice.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • Use AI to brainstorm and develop questions.
  • Evaluate AI-generated outlines and summaries.
  • Verify sources and factual claims.
  • Revise AI suggestions using your own words.

📝 What You Will Do

  • Improve an unclear or incomplete AI prompt.
  • Revise an AI-generated paragraph.
  • Identify unsupported citations.
  • Compare original and AI-assisted writing.
Continue to Module 4
⚖️

Module 4

Academic Integrity and AI Transparency

Responsible AI use means being honest about how a tool helped you. Learn how to distinguish appropriate assistance from plagiarism, substitution, and dishonest submission.

✅ Responsible AI Use

  • Using AI to brainstorm possible ideas.
  • Asking AI to explain a difficult concept.
  • Checking and revising AI-generated suggestions.
  • Acknowledging AI assistance when required.

❌ Irresponsible AI Use

  • Copying an AI response without understanding it.
  • Submitting AI-generated work as entirely your own.
  • Inventing references, experiences, or data.
  • Hiding AI use when disclosure is required.
Continue to Module 5
🔐

Module 5

Privacy, Safety, and Digital Responsibility

AI platforms may collect and process the information you provide. Learn what information should remain private and how to protect yourself and others online.

🟢 Safer to Share

  • General questions that do not identify you.
  • Fictional examples and practice scenarios.
  • Publicly available information.
  • Schoolwork with private details removed.

🔴 Keep Private

  • Passwords and account information.
  • Home addresses and identification numbers.
  • Private photos and confidential documents.
  • Personal information belonging to other people.
Continue to Module 6

Module 6

Responsible AI for Career Preparation

AI can support your transition from school to the workplace. Learn how to use it for career exploration, professional communication, résumé preparation, and interview practice.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • Use AI to explore possible careers.
  • Improve a résumé without inventing qualifications.
  • Draft professional emails and messages.
  • Practice answering interview questions.

💼 What You Will Do

  • Improve an AI-generated résumé profile.
  • Revise an unprofessional email.
  • Practice a mock interview response.
  • Evaluate AI-generated career advice.

Final Challenge

Create a responsible AI-assisted career output.

Apply what you learned by completing a career-related task with the support of an AI tool.

Performance Task

Show your process, not only your final answer.

Your work should demonstrate that you used AI critically, honestly, safely, and responsibly.

  1. Select a career-related task.
  2. Write and save your original AI prompt.
  3. Review the initial AI-generated response.
  4. Verify important information.
  5. Revise the output using your own voice.
  6. Explain how you used AI responsibly.
