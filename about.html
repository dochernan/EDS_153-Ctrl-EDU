About the Project

A learning shield for everyday digital life.

KALASAG is a mobile-first educational multimedia website designed to help Grade 12 learners protect personal data, secure online accounts, recognize digital threats, and respond safely when privacy or security incidents occur.

Our Purpose

From awareness to safer digital habits

KALASAG focuses on practical decisions that learners can apply immediately in school, at home, and across online platforms.

01

Protect personal data

Help learners distinguish public, personal, sensitive, and confidential information before sharing or uploading it.

02

Secure accounts

Build stronger habits involving passphrases, multi-factor authentication, account recovery, and device protection.

03

Recognize threats

Develop the judgment needed to identify phishing, impersonation, suspicious links, and social engineering.

04

Share with care

Encourage thoughtful decisions about audiences, app permissions, cloud files, photographs, and digital footprints.

05

Use AI more safely

Teach learners to minimize, anonymize, and protect personal or third-party information before prompting or uploading.

06

Respond and recover

Guide learners through calm, organized action after compromised accounts, lost devices, or accidental disclosures.

Main Learning Objective

What learners should be able to do

Learning Outcome

Recognize risks and choose appropriate protective actions.

Given realistic online scenarios, Grade 12 learners will identify privacy and security threats, distinguish safe from unsafe digital practices, and select appropriate protective or corrective actions with at least 80% accuracy.

  1. Identify personal and sensitive data.
  2. Secure accounts and devices.
  3. Recognize phishing and manipulation.
  4. Make safer sharing decisions.
  5. Protect data when using AI tools.
  6. Respond appropriately to incidents.

Why This Project

Designed from learner needs

The original front-end analysis showed strong digital access and a clear concern about personal data and online security.

94.3% Primarily use smartphones

This supports a responsive, touch-friendly, mobile-first learning experience.

97.1% Believe a mobile module can help

Learners are receptive to structured, interactive web-based instruction.

40% Selected privacy and security as a leading concern

This finding supported the decision to narrow the project scope to data privacy and digital security.

Scope note

The initial survey covered responsible AI and future-ready skills more broadly. A focused privacy-and-security diagnostic is recommended before final implementation to identify specific misconceptions and behaviors involving passwords, phishing, app permissions, AI uploads, and incident reporting.

Project Scope

What KALASAG covers

  • Personal, sensitive, and confidential information
  • Password, passphrase, and multi-factor authentication practices
  • Account recovery and active-session review
  • Phishing, scams, impersonation, and social engineering
  • Social-media audiences and digital footprints
  • App permissions, cloud files, and location sharing
  • Privacy when prompting or uploading to AI tools
  • Incident reporting, recovery, and personal action planning

The first version does not attempt to teach advanced cybersecurity, ethical hacking, network administration, malware analysis, or secure software engineering. Its focus is practical learner awareness and safer everyday behavior.

Design Principles

How the learning experience was designed

A

Mobile-first

Pages use responsive layouts, touch-friendly controls, short sections, and lightweight media.

B

Learner-centered

Content uses realistic student situations rather than abstract technical explanations.

C

Privacy-by-design

Activities do not request real credentials or sensitive information and use fictional examples.

D

Scenario-based

Learners analyze decisions involving messages, accounts, files, applications, photographs, and AI tools.

E

Accessible

The site uses readable typography, strong contrast, clear labels, keyboard-accessible controls, and concise language.

F

Action-oriented

Lessons end with checklists, practice tasks, reflection, and concrete protective actions.

Development Journey

How KALASAG was developed

1

Front-end analysis

Ethics and Privacy

How KALASAG handles learner information

Privacy-conscious implementation

KALASAG is designed as a front-end learning website. The current version does not require user registration and does not send assessment responses to a server.

  • No real passwords or verification codes should be entered.
  • Activities use fictional or anonymized examples.
  • Browser progress uses localStorage and remains device-specific.
  • Only minimum necessary evaluation data should be collected.
  • School procedures should guide incident reporting and support.
  • Content should be reviewed regularly as risks and platforms change.
