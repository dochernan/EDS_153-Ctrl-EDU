What KALASAG Stands For

KALASAG is built around one practical goal: helping learners make safer choices whenever they sign in, share, upload, click, or communicate online.

Why Digital Protection Matters

Every click, upload, login, and message can reveal something about you.

Digital safety is not only about avoiding hackers. It is also about controlling what you share, understanding where your data goes, and knowing how to act when a digital situation feels unsafe.

Choose the Safer Action

Good digital security is built through everyday decisions.

Safer Digital Habits

  • Use unique passphrases for important accounts.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication.
  • Check the sender and link before clicking.
  • Review app permissions and privacy settings.
  • Remove personal details before sharing files or prompts.

Risky Digital Habits

  • Reuse one password across several accounts.
  • Respond immediately to urgent or threatening messages.
  • Post private information publicly.
  • Allow every app permission without checking.
  • Upload confidential documents to unfamiliar platforms.

KALASAG Privacy Promise

Learn safely without giving away sensitive information.

KALASAG uses fictional scenarios and does not ask learners to submit real passwords, identification numbers, private files, or confidential account information.

  • No real passwords
  • No private documents
  • No unnecessary personal data
  • No public sharing of learner responses
