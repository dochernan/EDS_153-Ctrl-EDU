What GABAY Stands For

More than a name—GABAY is a responsible AI learning framework.

Each part of GABAY represents a skill or value students need to use artificial intelligence effectively in school, online spaces, and future workplaces.

G

Growing AI Literacy

Understand what AI can do, recognize its limitations, and use it as a learning tool rather than a shortcut.

B

Balance

Combine the speed of AI with human creativity, reasoning, empathy, and sound decision-making.

A

Accountability

Verify information, protect personal data, acknowledge AI assistance, and take ownership of final outputs.

Y

Youth Readiness

Build critical thinking, communication, digital literacy, and career skills for an AI-shaped future.

Why Responsible AI Matters

AI can support learning—but students must remain in control.

GABAY helps learners move beyond simply generating answers. It develops the judgment needed to question, verify, revise, and use AI with purpose.

🔍

Think Critically

✍️

Learn Authentically

🔐

Explore careers, improve application materials, and develop practical digital skills for future workplaces.

Choose Your Path

Responsible AI use begins with better choices.

✅ Guided by GABAY

  • Use AI to explore, practice, and improve.
  • Check facts using credible sources.
  • Revise outputs using your own judgment.
  • Protect personal and confidential information.
  • Acknowledge AI assistance when required.

⚠️ Unguided AI Use

  • Copy answers without understanding them.
  • Trust every AI-generated claim immediately.
  • Submit generated work as entirely original.
  • Share private or sensitive information.
  • Use AI to deceive, harm, or misrepresent.

Your GABAY Check

Pause before you use an AI response.

Use these questions to make responsible decisions before submitting, sharing, or acting on AI-generated content.

  1. Do I understand the response?
  2. Have I checked whether it is accurate?
  3. Did I verify important claims using credible sources?
  4. Does the final output reflect my own thinking?
  5. Did I protect personal and private information?
  6. Am I being honest about how I used AI?
